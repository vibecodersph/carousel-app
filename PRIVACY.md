# Privacy Policy for carousel-app

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Vibe Coders PH uses `carousel-app` as an internal content-publishing tool for
Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts that Vibe Coders PH owns or
administers.

## Information we process

The app may process Meta account and Page identifiers, access tokens granted by
authorized administrators, publishing status, post identifiers, permalinks, and
content-performance data made available through Meta APIs. The app does not
offer public Facebook Login and does not intentionally collect information from
followers or other members of the public.

## How we use information

We use this information only to create, schedule, publish, verify, and measure
content on accounts administered by Vibe Coders PH; maintain operational logs;
and troubleshoot publishing failures.

## Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with third
parties except Meta and infrastructure providers needed to operate the app, or
when required by law.

## Storage, security, and retention

Credentials and operational records are restricted to authorized operators.
We retain them only while needed to operate the publishing workflow, meet legal
requirements, or resolve security and operational issues. Access can be revoked
at any time through Meta's Business settings.

## Data deletion

To request deletion of information associated with this app, email
vibecodersph26@gmail.com with the subject `carousel-app data deletion`. We will
verify the request, remove data we control that is no longer required, and
confirm completion. Administrators can also revoke the app's access in Meta's
Business settings.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. The
effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
